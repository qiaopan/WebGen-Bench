Our first attempt used text lessons. On tasks 1 to 30, it scored 39.3 percent, against 26 percent. But on tasks 61 to 101, it fell to 22.9 percent, below the baseline. The reason: vague advice, like make sure the form works, filled the prompt, and the small model could not act on it.

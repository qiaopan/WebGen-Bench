Here is the setup, step by step. One: the Bolt.diy agent with WebGen-LM-7B builds the website, with one try and no repair. Two: the WebVoyager agent with Qwen 32B tests the features. Three: Qwen 32B also grades a screenshot for appearance; the paper used GPT-4o. Four: our memory module uses Qwen 72B and the bge-m3 embedding. Accuracy counts a partial pass as one half. Our No Memory baseline is 28.5 percent, and the paper reports 28.4 percent.
